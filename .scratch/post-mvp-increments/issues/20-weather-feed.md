# Weather feed for hot-conditions hydration

Status: backlog
Proposed increment: 2

Source: ruled out of the 8-week MVP in Nutrition and hydration logic (`.scratch/mvp-plan/issues/17-nutrition-hydration-logic.md`).

## What

Pull local temperature/humidity for planned and logged outdoor Activities so the hot-conditions water and electrolyte adjustments apply without the manual toggle.

## Why not in the MVP

The MVP uses a one-tap 'hot conditions' toggle (pre-ticked for kite, windsurf, beach volleyball in summer); free weather APIs' commercial-use terms are unverified.

## Depends on

Research on weather API licensing (commercial use, EU data); Nutrition and hydration logic.
