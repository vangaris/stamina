# Weekly re-plan Adaptation

Status: backlog
Proposed increment: 1

Source: ruled out of the 8-week MVP in MVP scope per Pillar, including Nutrition depth (`.scratch/mvp-plan/issues/12-mvp-scope.md`).

## What

Once a week Stamina reviews the whole week (Sessions, Activities, Check-ins, meal Logs) and suggests a re-plan of the next week.

## Why not in the MVP

Cut in the MVP capacity check (−3 h): the MVP only suggests Adaptations after an Activity or a poor Check-in.

## Depends on

Training generation model; Generation engine.
