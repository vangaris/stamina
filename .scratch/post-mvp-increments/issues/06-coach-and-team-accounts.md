# Coach dashboard and team accounts

Status: backlog
Proposed increment: 3

Source: ruled out of the 8-week MVP in MVP scope per Pillar, including Nutrition depth (`.scratch/mvp-plan/issues/12-mvp-scope.md`).

## What

A coach or team manager sees and plans for a squad; team-level Activities (practices, games) sync to players.

## Why not in the MVP

B2B surface beyond the ICP's solo use; MVP treats team practices as Activities the player logs.

## Depends on

Monetization and pricing (B2B tier); Data model (multi-user access, roles).
