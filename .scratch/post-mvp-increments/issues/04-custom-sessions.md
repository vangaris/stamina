# Custom Sessions built by the user

Status: backlog
Proposed increment: 3

Source: ruled out of the 8-week MVP in MVP scope per Pillar, including Nutrition depth (`.scratch/mvp-plan/issues/12-mvp-scope.md`).

## What

Users build or edit their own Sessions and have Stamina count them in load and Adaptation.

## Why not in the MVP

MVP Sessions are suggested by Stamina and checked against progression, Deload and safety rules; user-built Sessions need the same validation path.

## Depends on

Training generation model; Generation engine.
