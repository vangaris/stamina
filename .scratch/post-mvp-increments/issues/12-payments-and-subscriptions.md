# Payments and subscriptions

Status: backlog
Proposed increment: 1

Source: ruled out of the 8-week MVP in MVP scope per Pillar, including Nutrition depth (`.scratch/mvp-plan/issues/12-mvp-scope.md`).

## What

Charge for Stamina per Monetization and pricing (`.scratch/mvp-plan/issues/13-monetization.md`). That covers the €7.99/month or €59.99/year VAT-inclusive price everywhere, the 14-day no-card trial with a reminder 2 days before the end, one-tap cancel, and read-only access when lapsed. It also covers the founding offer (€39.99 first year, upfront, within 30 days) and Paddle as merchant of record (eligibility confirmed up front; Stripe direct fallback). Store billing applies if the app ships in stores. Prerequisite **[Vang]**: register έναρξη εργασιών the week this ships.

## Why not in the MVP

The MVP is a free invite-only beta so all hours go to proving sport-aware Adaptation. Charging starts only when the week-4 retention target is met and ≥ 30 beta users said they would pay.

## Depends on

Monetization and pricing; What does a Greek solo founder need to sell EU consumer subscriptions (`.scratch/mvp-plan/issues/30-selling-subscriptions-from-greece.md`); delivery platform (store billing rules).
