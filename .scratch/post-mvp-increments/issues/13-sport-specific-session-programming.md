# Sport-specific Session programming

Status: backlog
Proposed increment: 3

Source: ruled out of the 8-week MVP in MVP scope per Pillar, including Nutrition depth (`.scratch/mvp-plan/issues/12-mvp-scope.md`).

## What

Stamina writes the sport itself as Sessions: running plans (intervals, long runs), CrossFit-style workouts, HYROX station work, calisthenics skill progressions.

## Why not in the MVP

In the MVP these sports are Activities the user does; Stamina's Sessions support them (strength, conditioning, mobility) and count them in load. A new Session type per sport is too much for 8 weeks.

## Depends on

Training generation model; sport catalogue.
