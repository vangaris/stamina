# Adaptive energy targets from weight trend

Status: backlog
Proposed increment: 2

Source: ruled out of the 8-week MVP in Nutrition and hydration logic (`.scratch/mvp-plan/issues/17-nutrition-hydration-logic.md`).

## What

Learn each user's real energy expenditure from their logged intake proxy and weekly weight trend, and adjust targets continuously.

## Why not in the MVP

The MVP recalculates targets only on a ≥ 2 kg weight change or a Goal/schedule change; adaptive estimation needs more data and validation.

## Depends on

Nutrition and hydration logic; weekly weigh-ins.
