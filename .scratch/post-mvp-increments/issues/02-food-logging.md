# Food logging, food database and barcode scan

Status: backlog
Proposed increment: 2

Source: ruled out of the 8-week MVP in MVP scope per Pillar, including Nutrition depth (`.scratch/mvp-plan/issues/12-mvp-scope.md`).

## What

Let users log what they actually ate against their targets, search a food database and scan barcodes; feeds Nutrition Adaptation with real intake.

## Why not in the MVP

MVP Nutrition is targets + AI meal suggestions with a one-tap meal Log; full logging competes with MyFitnessPal and needs a food data source (licensing, Greek foods).

## Depends on

Nutrition and hydration logic; food data source choice (open/USDA/Greek composition tables).
