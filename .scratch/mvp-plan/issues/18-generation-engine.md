Type: grilling
Status: open

## Question

Does Stamina generate Programs with deterministic rules, an LLM, or a hybrid (e.g. rules produce the Program, LLM only explains/personalizes copy)? Where exactly can the LLM act, what validates its output against the safety rules, what health data (if any) may leave the backend to an LLM provider, how is per-user LLM cost capped, and how is the engine tested and versioned so Adaptations are reproducible?
