# Who is the first user, and how is Stamina positioned?

Type: grilling
Status: claimed
Blocked by: 03

## Question

Who is the ideal customer profile for the first 100 users (sport, level, age band, geography, equipment, schedule, willingness to pay), what problem does Stamina solve for them that they currently solve badly, and what is the one-line positioning against Fitbod, MyFitnessPal and Future (see the competitor teardown ticket)? Output: ICP statement, problem statement, positioning statement, and the anti-persona (who Stamina is explicitly not for).

## Comments

- 2026-10-28 round 1 (Vang accepted all recommendations):
  - ICP segment: recreational competitors, adults playing a weekly sport with games/matches (basketball, 5-a-side, padel, volleyball, tennis) who want training and eating fitted around them. Not general gym-goers, weight-loss-only, or endurance.
  - Level: intermediate (≥ 1 year active).
  - Age: 18+ hard floor; focus 25–45.
  - Geography: first 100 in Greece (Athens), EN/EL; EU-open; no US marketing until post-MVP.
  - Team players with a coach are in: team practices and games are Activities; Stamina fills strength, nutrition and recovery gaps ("training, nutrition, or both").
  - Design target: gym or basic home kit; 2–4 Sessions × 30–60 min/week plus 1–3 games.
  - Problem: no plan reacts to their game schedule; generic apps don't know a game happened; they stitch Fitbod, MFP, YouTube and guesswork.
  - WTP hypothesis: €5–10/month (below Fitbod €17.99; padel S&C €10–20).
  - Anti-persona: screened out: under-18, pregnant, injury rehab, known cardiovascular/metabolic/renal disease; not targeted: physique bodybuilders, strict calorie trackers, elite athletes with staff.
