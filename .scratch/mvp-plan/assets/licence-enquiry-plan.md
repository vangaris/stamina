## Outcomes and what happens next

| Outcome | Meaning | What Safety rules does with it |
|---|---|---|
| **Free permission** | Written authorization, no fee | Embed that instrument unmodified (EN). Record the licence term and attribution text |
| **Fee quoted** | Permission at a price | Weigh the fee against €0 until traction. If adopted it's a first paid threshold; otherwise use the fallback |
| **Link-out allowed** (PAR-Q+ only) | Users can complete the official online PAR-Q+ and confirm the result in the app | Candidate route with no licence: costs onboarding drop-off, needs no permission |
| **Refused** or **No reply** | No usable permission by Day 14 | Fallback: Stamina writes its own screening questions from the public ACSM screening logic and the red-flag list in the screening research. It does **not** call them "PAR-Q+" or "validated". Flag it for the Legal review fog item |

Late replies after Day 14 still get recorded. If a free permission arrives later, Safety rules can switch to the licensed instrument without re-planning.
