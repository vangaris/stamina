# Stamina

A coaching product that generates and adapts a personalized Program for each user from their goals, sport, equipment, schedule and feedback.

## Language

### The plan

**Program**:
Everything generated for one user across all four Pillars, spanning one or more Blocks.
_Avoid_: Plan, routine, schedule

**Pillar**:
One of the four domains a Program covers: Training, Nutrition, Mobility & Recovery, Daily Routine.
_Avoid_: Module, area, track

**Block**:
A multi-week phase of a Program with a single training emphasis.
_Avoid_: Mesocycle, phase, cycle

**Session**:
One Training or Mobility & Recovery workout that Stamina schedules on a given day.
_Avoid_: Workout, class, day

**Goal**:
The single primary outcome a user picks for their Program: perform better in their sport, get stronger, general fitness, or body composition.
_Avoid_: Objective, target, aim

**Sport Profile**:
The approved description of a sport's demands that the engine plans around: energy mix, key movements, high-load body areas, typical Activity, recovery window and season.
_Avoid_: Sport module, sport template, sport program

### Feedback

**Check-in**:
A user's daily readiness report: sleep, soreness and perceived exertion.
_Avoid_: Survey, daily log, readiness score

**Log**:
The record of what a user actually did in a Session or ate in a meal.
_Avoid_: Entry, history, tracking

**Screening**:
The yes/no health questions a user answers before getting a Program, whose outcome is cleared, an excluded body area, or referred to a professional.
_Avoid_: PAR-Q (unless licensed), assessment, medical check, clearance

**Consent**:
A separate, explicit, unticked-by-default agreement the user gives, recorded with its purpose, text version and language; the wellness disclaimer and the processing of health data are two consents, and neither is implied by the other.
_Avoid_: Terms acceptance, opt-in, agreement

**Referred**:
The Screening outcome that asks the user to talk to a professional before any Program; it names no condition, and for pregnancy the wording mentions a doctor or midwife.
_Avoid_: Rejected, failed, not cleared, high risk

**Limited mode**:
How training starts after a Referred user states "A doctor has said I can train": effort capped at RPE 7, no power or plyometric slot, no RIR 1; Stamina records the statement and never issues clearance. Pregnancy has no Limited mode.
_Avoid_: Safe mode, medical clearance, light mode

**Stop flag**:
A symptom the user reports during a Check-in or Session that pauses training and points them to medical help.
_Avoid_: Red flag alert, warning, symptom check

**Activity**:
Sport the user does outside a Session, planned or not, such as a basketball game, a padel match or a windy kitesurfing afternoon, logged manually or imported from a health app.
_Avoid_: Game, match, external workout, sport session

**Load**:
The training stress of a Session or Activity, scored as perceived exertion (RPE) times minutes, and summed per week.
_Avoid_: Volume, strain, training stress score

**Adaptation**:
A suggested change to a Program, triggered by Logs, Check-ins or Activities, that the user accepts or declines.
_Avoid_: Adjustment, regeneration, update

**Deload**:
A planned short period of reduced training load inside a Block, scheduled or suggested as an Adaptation.
_Avoid_: Recovery week, rest week
