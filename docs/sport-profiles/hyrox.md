## Uncertain, for founder review

- Energy mix is an estimate; no source gives percentages for HYROX.
- Recovery window is a coaching heuristic.
- METs cover only the running component. The running legs alone are about 9.0; stations have no MET entry, so `hard` (10.5) is a judgement and may overstate average energy cost.
- Season months follow [4] (October to the June World Championships); the calendar lists no Greek event. The World Championships are qualification-only.
- [2] is one small study (n = 11, mostly men, one simulated race in a gym).
- Station weights vary by division; the profile assumes the Open division.
