Sources are numbered as in the file: [1] Compendium water activities; [2] "Physiological assessment of Olympic windsurfers" (2015); [3] "Electromyographic activity during windsurfing on water" (1996); [4] "Etesian" (Wikipedia).

## Uncertain, for the founder's review

- Sources 2 and 3 study Olympic-level or small-sample windsurfers (6 subjects in source 3, light to moderate wind). A recreational session is probably less demanding; the energy mix and hard METs may be high for recreational riders.
- Hard METs (11.0) is a crossing trial value shared with kitesurfing; the Compendium's 14.0 competition value is deliberately left out.
- I read abstracts only, not full texts.
- Source 4 is an encyclopaedia article on the Etesian winds, not a climatology; it gives the mid-May to mid-September window only.
- The 60 min duration and both recovery values are heuristics.
