## Uncertain, for the founder's review

- Sources 2 and 3 study elite or professional players; recreational games are less intense, so the energy mix may over-weight sprint and explosive effort for recreational players.
- The high-load areas are an inference, not a cited statement.
- Key movements has only two entries; the cited data does not support others (for example overhead or push).
- Season follows a professional European league; recreational leagues in Greece may run on a different calendar. Playoff months (May) are left out.
- I read abstracts (sources 2, 3) and the date range of the calendar PDF (source 4), not full texts.
- Duration and recovery hours are heuristics.
